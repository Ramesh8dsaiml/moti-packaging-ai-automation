## 3. Technical Guardrails Protocol (Preventing AI Hallucinations)

To prevent the LLM from making inaccurate technical claims or committing to non-viable manufacturing parameters, the workflow enforces a **3-tier guardrail system**:

1. **Deterministic System Boundary:** The LLM prompt explicitly restricts the scope to composite paper cans, rigid paper tubes, and core containers. Any requests for plastic extrusion, tinplate drums, or glass jars are strictly classified as `Not currently serviceable`.
2. **Zero-Hallucination Policy on Tooling Dimensions:** The model is prohibited from guessing or approximating missing diameters or heights. If a dimension is missing, it is extracted as `null`, flagged in `missing_fields`, and routed to `More information required`.
3. **Human-in-the-Loop Threshold:** High-volume orders (>50,000 units), custom barrier requests (boil-proof, liquid packaging), or unusual dimensions automatically trigger `human_review_required = true`, halting automated pricing commitments until vetted by senior plant engineers.
